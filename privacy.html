Word Quest – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026 · 中文版本

Word Quest (“the App”) is a vocabulary and spelling practice app for students in grades K–12. It is provided by [YOUR NAME] (“we”). This policy explains what the App does and does not do with information.

Short version

We do not collect, store on our servers, sell, or share any personal information. The App has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no in-app purchases.

What stays on your device

To remember your progress, the App saves your game data (XP, streak, grade level, and which words you have practiced) in your device’s local storage. This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. You can erase it at any time by clearing the App’s storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, ages, photos, contacts, location, device identifiers, or microphone or camera access. The App does not use cookies for tracking.

Speech feature

The Spelling Bee and pronunciation buttons use the text-to-speech engine built into your device to read words aloud. The words are not sent to us. Depending on your device’s settings, your device’s speech engine may handle this itself under its own privacy terms.

Optional dictionary lookup

On the “Add my own words” screen you can tap “Look up meaning.” Only if you do, the App sends the single word you typed to the free Free Dictionary API (dictionaryapi.dev) to get its meaning. No other information is sent, and we do not receive it. That service may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) under its own policies. You can skip this feature and type meanings yourself; the rest of the App works without it.

Hosting

The App’s files are delivered over the internet from GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) under its own privacy statement. We do not receive or use those logs. After the first load, the App can run offline.

Children’s privacy

Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the applicable age in your country). The App contains no advertising, no links to social networks, and no chat or user-generated content. The only link leads to this policy.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Any change that affects how information is handled will be described here before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions? Email [YOUR EMAIL].

Word Quest 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 8 日

Word Quest（“本应用”）是面向 K–12 年级学生的词汇和拼写练习应用，由 [你的姓名]（“我们”）提供。本政策说明本应用如何处理信息。

简要说明

我们不收集、不在服务器上存储、不出售、也不分享任何个人信息。本应用没有账号、没有广告、没有数据分析，也没有内购。

保存在你设备上的内容

为了记住学习进度，本应用会把游戏数据（经验值、连续天数、年级和已练习的单词）保存在设备本地存储中。这些数据不会离开你的设备，我们也看不到。你可以随时在设备设置中清除本应用的存储空间，或卸载本应用来删除这些数据。

我们不收集的信息

我们不会索取或收集姓名、邮箱、年龄、照片、通讯录、位置、设备标识符，也不会使用麦克风或摄像头。本应用不使用 Cookie 进行跟踪。

语音功能

拼写比赛和发音按钮使用设备自带的文字转语音引擎朗读单词。这些单词不会发送给我们。根据设备设置，语音引擎可能按其自身的隐私条款处理朗读请求。

可选的词典查询

在“添加我的单词”页面，你可以点击“Look up meaning”。只有在你点击时，本应用才会把你输入的这一个单词发送给免费的 Free Dictionary API（dictionaryapi.dev）以获取释义。不会发送其他任何信息，我们也不会收到。该服务可能按其自身政策保留标准技术日志（例如 IP 地址）。你可以不使用这个功能，自己输入释义；本应用其余功能不依赖它。

托管服务

本应用的文件通过 GitHub Pages 提供。与任何网站托管服务一样，GitHub 可能按其自身隐私声明保留标准技术日志（例如 IP 地址）。我们不会接收或使用这些日志。首次加载后，本应用可以离线运行。

儿童隐私

由于我们不收集任何人的个人信息，因此也不会有意收集 13 岁以下儿童（或所在国家/地区规定年龄以下儿童）的个人信息。本应用没有广告、没有社交网络链接，也没有聊天或用户生成内容。应用内唯一的链接指向本政策。

政策变更

如果我们修改本政策，会更新上方日期。任何影响信息处理方式的变更，都会在生效前在此说明。
